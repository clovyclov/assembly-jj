Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Assembly Jiu Jitsu ("we," "our," or "us"), located at 281 Enterprise Ct Suite 400, Bloomfield Township, MI 48302, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website (go.assembly-detroit.com) and communication channels.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, submit a form, or communicate with our team.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when claiming a free class pass, scheduling an appointment, or contacting us:

  • Contact Data: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Program Preferences: Age range and target audience (e.g., Adults BJJ, Kids Martial Arts).
  • Automated Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and website usage data collected via cookies and analytics pixels (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information collected for the following purposes:

  • To process your request for a free trial class or schedule an appointment.
  • To send confirmation messages, schedule updates, and appointment reminders via SMS, phone, or email.
  • To provide customer support and answer inquiries regarding our programs and location.
  • To optimize our website, advertising performance, and user experience.

4. SMS Messaging & Mobile Privacy Policy

Assembly Jiu Jitsu strictly adheres to A2P 10DLC mobile messaging compliance guidelines:

🔒 Mobile Information Privacy Protection:

"No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties."

  • Opt-In Consent: By providing your phone number on our forms, you explicitly consent to receive transactional and marketing text messages from Assembly Jiu Jitsu. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
  • Opt-Out Instructions: You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message received. For help, reply HELP or call us at (248) 413-8386.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary data with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving our students (such as customer management platforms and web hosting services), provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
