No prior experience is necessary! Our fundamentals classes are designed specifically for beginners. You will learn foundational positions, escapes, and basic techniques step-by-step in a safe, controlled environment.

For your first class, wear comfortable athletic apparel like a fitted t-shirt or rashguard and shorts or athletic leggings without zippers or metal snaps. Bring a bottle of water, and we'll take care of the rest!
