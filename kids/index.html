Frequently Asked Questions (Parents)

Everything you need to know about your child's first class at Assembly.

What age can my child start Jiu-Jitsu?

Our youth program starts at age 4! We separate classes by age and skill group (Little Champions 4-7 and Youth 8-13) to ensure age-appropriate coaching and maximum safety.

Will my child get hurt?

Safety is our #1 priority. Our world-class black belt instructors closely supervise every drill and sparring session in a controlled, non-violent environment.

What should my child wear to their first class?

Comfortable athletic clothes like t-shirt and shorts/sweatpants (no zippers or metal buttons). We provide loaner gear for your free trial pass!

What if my child has zero prior martial arts experience?

No experience is required! 90%+ of our new kids start as complete beginners. Our coaches guide them step-by-step at their own pace.
